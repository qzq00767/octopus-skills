# Photo Revival

Reimagine the uploaded photograph as a poetic hand-drawn illustration. Preserve the recognizable subject, setting and one or two memorable details, while simplifying and redrawing the scene rather than applying a photo filter.

## Composition and style

- Use white or very light paper with generous empty space.
- Concentrate the subject in a small illustrated cluster, normally around 10-16% of the canvas and no more than 18%. Leave roughly 80-88% as blank paper.
- Draw with delicate pencil edges, brush marks, paper texture or watercolor softness.
- Keep vivid, controlled color inside the small illustrated area.
- Tiny collage fragments, stamps or clippings may be used sparingly when integrated with the illustration.
- Optional handwritten captions should be tiny and understated. Do not invent personal names, dates or facts; use user-provided text when available.

## Parameter priority

The user's explicit instructions and image request parameters take priority over these defaults. Preserve the requested aspect ratio, dimensions, resolution, output format and background setting. Never replace an explicit aspect ratio with the skill's preferred layout.

Only when the user leaves composition and aspect ratio automatic, prefer a 3:4 vertical composition. Adapt the same small-subject, generous-space treatment to any other requested ratio.

## Avoid

Avoid full-bleed photographs, filter effects, realistic beauty retouching, flat vector graphics, dense layouts, large collage blocks and oversized poster text.

Generate the resulting illustration using the reference photo and these directions. Do not put an explanation or the generation prompt into the image.
